# Teacher Dashboard

## Role
Teacher manages only assigned classes, students, subjects, timetable, attendance, marks, and reports.

## Sidebar
```text
Dashboard
My Classes
My Students
My Timetable
Attendance
Exams & Marks
Student Reports
Notifications
My Profile
```

## Dashboard statistics
- My Classes
- My Students
- Today's Classes
- Attendance Pending
- Upcoming Exams
- Pending Marks
- Reports Pending/Submitted

Today's schedule: Time, Class, Subject, Room, Status.

Quick actions: Take Attendance, Enter Marks, Create Student Report.

## My Classes
Table: Class, Subject, Students, Next class, Attendance status.
Detail: Students, Subject, Timetable, Attendance, Marks, Reports.

## My Students
Filters: Class, Attendance status, Search.
Columns: Student, Class, Attendance today, Recent mark, Report status.
Only assigned students are accessible.

## My Timetable
Today and week views with Time, Class, Subject, Location, Status.

## Attendance
Select class/date/session. Mark:
- Present
- Absent
- Late
- Excused

Submit and preserve audit history.

## Exams & Marks
Views: Upcoming, Open mark entry, Completed.
Fields: Student, Score, Comment.
System calculates total/average/grade where configured.

## Student Reports
Create, Save Draft, Submit, and send to parent/admin/both according to school policy.
Fields: Student, Category, Report, Date, Priority, Recipient.
Categories: Academic, Behaviour, Attendance, Achievement, Concern, General.

## Notifications
Admin messages, schedule changes, class announcements, report feedback, exam announcements.

## My Profile
Name, photo, email, phone, assigned subjects/classes, password/security.

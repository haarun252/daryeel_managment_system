# Parent Dashboard

## Role
Parent can see only their own children and information released by the school.

## Sidebar
```text
Dashboard
My Children
Attendance
Timetable
Teachers
Exams & Marks
Reports
Fees & Payments
Transport
Notifications
My Profile
```

## Dashboard
Child selector for parents with multiple children.

### Statistics per child
- Attendance rate
- Present days
- Absent days
- Upcoming classes/exams
- Outstanding fee
- Latest published average/mark

Today: attendance, timetable, teacher, important notifications.

Quick actions: Attendance, Marks, Report, Fees, Transport.

## My Children
Cards: Photo, Name, Class, Attendance status, Fee status, Bus/route.
Detail: Overview, Enrollment, Attendance, Timetable, Teachers, Exams & Marks, Reports, Fees, Payments, Transport.

## Attendance
Filters: child, date range, status.
Statistics: rate, present, absent, late.
Show calendar/list.

## Timetable
Weekly schedule for selected child: Subject, Teacher, Time, Location.

## Teachers
Only teachers assigned to the selected child. Show name, subject, class, official contact if permitted.

## Exams & Marks
Current and previous terms.
Table: Subject, Exam, Score, Grade, Teacher comment if published.
Only published marks are visible.

## Reports
Only released reports.
Show date, teacher, category, content, status.

## Fees & Payments
Statistics: Current outstanding, this month's fee, paid, partial, overdue.
Fee table: Month, Fee, Paid, Remaining, Status, Due date.
Payment history: Receipt, Date, Amount, Method.
Optional future online payment: Pay now, status, transaction reference.

## Transport
Show selected child's Bus, Route, Pickup location/time, Drop-off location, Assigned days.
Never expose another child's location.

## Notifications
Tabs: All, Attendance, Fees, Exams, Reports, Transport, School announcements.
Channels: In-app, WhatsApp, Email.

## My Profile
Name, phone, email, children, address where appropriate, emergency contact, password/security, notification preferences.
